Ice rink is the exception: crewed by Lindy, no bike wash, no cooking. 45 minutes, not 75.

This is a solo ITT, not the Mega Epic — which changes the support picture. Registered Mega riders may use Epic's aid stations. You are riding the same 217 miles independently, so assume no course support at all: no aid stations, no water drops, no feed zones. Your resupply is exactly three points — the house twice, the ice rink once — plus streams. That makes the Sawyer core equipment rather than a backup, and it means every past finisher below had a materially easier day than the one drawn here.

One practical consequence: stage 3 has three aid stations across 40.5 miles and stage 4 has three across 42.3. Riding those dry on a 0.8 L bottle means the filter comes out several times per stage. Worth knowing your water sources on Guyot and Aqueduct before Wednesday — that's map work, not riding, so it costs the taper nothing.
